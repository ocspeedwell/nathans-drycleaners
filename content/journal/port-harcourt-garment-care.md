## Serving GRA Phase 2

The Nathans Port Harcourt branch is at 67 Woji Road, GRA Phase 2. Customers can enquire about laundry and dry cleaning, pressing and other available services directly with the branch.

## Special garments need extra detail

Describe embroidery, beads, silk-like fabrics, tailored construction and any stains. This helps the team assess the item before recommending treatment.

## Plan ahead

Confirm the current price, estimated turnaround and whether collection is available for your location. Avoid assuming that services or timings are identical across branches.

## When to ask a professional

Garment labels, dyes, embellishments and previous cleaning treatments can change the safest approach. If an item is valuable, delicate or heavily stained, seek a garment-specific assessment rather than experimenting with harsh chemicals or heat.
