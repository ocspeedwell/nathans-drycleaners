About Nathans

Proudly Nigerian. Trusted Since 1997.

Since 1997, Nathans Dry Cleaners has cared for the clothes Nigerians wear to work, to celebrate and to make lasting impressions. We understand that every garment carries a story.

From everyday essentials to treasured occasion wear, we combine thoughtful service with careful garment handling across our Nigerian branch network.
