Your Wuse II branch

The Nathans Abuja branch is at 10 Lobito Crescent, Wuse II. Customers can contact the branch for garment-care enquiries and confirmation of current services.

Explain what you need

Mention whether the items are suits, everyday clothing, delicate occasion wear or household textiles. If you need a particular completion date, ask the team to confirm feasibility before handing over garments.

Get current information

Ask for the current quote and any available pickup arrangements directly. Cleaning requirements vary by fabric and condition, so the final recommendation may depend on inspection.

When to ask a professional

Garment labels, dyes, embellishments and previous cleaning treatments can change the safest approach. If an item is valuable, delicate or heavily stained, seek a garment-specific assessment rather than experimenting with harsh chemicals or heat.
