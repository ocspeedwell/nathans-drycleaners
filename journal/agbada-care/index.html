How to Care for Your Agbada Without Damaging Its Embroidery

Garment care guidance from Nathans Dry Cleaners, serving Nigeria since 1997.

Agbada often combines structured fabric with delicate embroidery. Check the care label, avoid scrubbing decorative threads and have heavily embellished garments assessed before cleaning.

For storage, use a broad hanger and breathable garment cover. Never apply heat directly to intricate embroidery. A professional cleaner can assess colourfastness and the safest treatment for each garment.

Start with the construction

Agbada can combine a flowing outer robe, embroidered neckline, matching trousers and a cap. Different components may use different fabrics and dyes, so do not assume they can all be washed together. Inspect the neckline, cuffs and embroidery for loose threads before cleaning.

Treat spills conservatively

Blot fresh liquid with a clean white cloth. Do not scrub decorative stitching or apply bleach, detergent paste or stain removers without checking compatibility. If a stain contains oil, ink or dye, explain its origin to a professional cleaner.

Press and store carefully

Avoid placing a hot iron directly on embroidery, beads or metallic threads. Use a pressing cloth only when permitted by the care label. Store the garment fully dry on a supportive hanger or folded with tissue to reduce hard creases. Air it after wearing before long-term storage.

When to ask a professional

Garment labels, dyes, embellishments and previous cleaning treatments can change the safest approach. If an item is valuable, delicate or heavily stained, seek a garment-specific assessment rather than experimenting with harsh chemicals or heat.
