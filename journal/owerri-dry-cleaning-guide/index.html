Dry Cleaning in Owerri: What to Ask

Nathans has two Owerri branches, at MCC Road and New Owerri.

Choose the most convenient branch and confirm garment requirements, current prices, turnaround and any pickup arrangements directly.

Choose between two branches

Nathans has an MCC Road branch at 32 MCC/Uratta Road and a New Owerri branch at 26 EC Iwuanyanwu Avenue. Choose the location that is most convenient for your handover or enquiry.

Ask the right questions

Tell the team what you need cleaned, the number of items, whether there are stains or embellishments and when you hope to collect them. Ask which services are available at your selected branch.

Confirm prices with the branch

Prices and turnaround times can change. Request a current quote and confirm pickup arrangements directly rather than relying on figures copied from older lists.

For professional garment care and current quotations, contact your preferred Nathans branch.
