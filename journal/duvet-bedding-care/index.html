Duvet and Bedding Care Guide

Clean bedding supports a comfortable home, but bulky duvets need sufficient washing and drying capacity.

Follow the sewn-in care label and dry thoroughly before storage to prevent musty odours. Professional cleaning may be appropriate for oversized or delicate fills.

Read the filling label

Duvets may contain down, feathers, synthetic fibres or other fills. Each material has different washing and drying requirements. A duvet that fits in a machine when dry may still be too bulky to wash and rinse effectively.

Drying is essential

Trapped moisture can create unpleasant odours and may encourage microbial growth. Follow the manufacturer's drying instructions and check that the filling is dry throughout, not just on the surface, before making the bed or storing it.

Plan a sensible routine

Use removable duvet covers, attend to spills promptly and wash bedding according to use, allergies and the manufacturer's guidance. Ask a professional cleaner about bulky or specialist items.

When to ask a professional

Garment labels, dyes, embellishments and previous cleaning treatments can change the safest approach. If an item is valuable, delicate or heavily stained, seek a garment-specific assessment rather than experimenting with harsh chemicals or heat.
