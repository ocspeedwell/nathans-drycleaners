Nathans Locations & Services

Laundry Pickup Enquiries in Lagos

For customers in Victoria Island, Nathans provides a direct branch contact for garment-care enquiries.

Describe the items, quantities and preferred collection area to the branch team. Confirm pickup availability and turnaround directly before making arrangements.

Contact the Victoria Island team

Nathans' Victoria Island branch is located at 56 Adetokunbo Ademola Street. When contacting the team, mention the garment type, quantity, stain concerns and the area where collection may be required.

Confirm arrangements directly

Pickup availability, service suitability, turnaround and prices can change. Ask the branch to confirm these details before making plans. Do not rely on an old online price list.

Prepare items for handover

Check pockets, separate items requiring special attention and describe pre-existing damage or difficult stains. Keep a simple item count for your records.

When to ask a professional

Garment labels, dyes, embellishments and previous cleaning treatments can change the safest approach. If an item is valuable, delicate or heavily stained, seek a garment-specific assessment rather than experimenting with harsh chemicals or heat.

For professional garment care and current quotations, contact your preferred Nathans branch.
