Caring for Lace and Aso-Oke

Nigerian occasion wear deserves fabric-specific care, particularly when beads, metallic threads or layered fabrics are involved.

Check embellishments before treatment, avoid wringing and store pieces in a dry, ventilated place. Ask for an assessment before attempting stain removal.

Check decoration and colour

Lace and aso-oke vary widely in weave, dye stability and ornamentation. Check for loose beads, glued stones, metallic yarn and contrasting colours before any cleaning. A hidden-area colourfastness assessment may be needed.

Avoid rough washing

Do not wring, aggressively brush or soak embellished garments without professional advice. Some structured pieces can distort when wet, while others have trims that cannot withstand solvent cleaning.

Store with care

Allow garments to dry fully before storing. Keep heavy embellishments from pulling on fragile fabric, use breathable covers and avoid prolonged exposure to sunlight or damp conditions.

When to ask a professional

Garment labels, dyes, embellishments and previous cleaning treatments can change the safest approach. If an item is valuable, delicate or heavily stained, seek a garment-specific assessment rather than experimenting with harsh chemicals or heat.
